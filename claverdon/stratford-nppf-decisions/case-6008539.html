Case summary

Land adjacent to Fulwood, School Road, Bagnall

Appeal 6008539 · Staffordshire Moorlands District Council · Planning Inspector · decided 18 August 2026 · dismissed

A bungalow with garage at the edge of Bagnall village, in the setting of the Bagnall Conservation Area, in the Green Belt.

Case page · Decision letter

Cited in: 2. Heritage harm at Forest Farm

Why this is cited. This appeal shows that a favourable Green Belt finding does not settle the case: the grey belt finding “does not in itself justify the grant”, and the heritage harm still had to be weighed against the public benefits on its own terms. Relevant to Station Road, where the applicant relies on grey belt.

Key points

  • Heritage: "the proposal would not provide sufficient public benefits that would outweigh the harm" (¶13).
  • Green Belt: the site was grey belt, so not inappropriate, but "that finding does not in itself justify the grant of planning permission, nor does it override the identified harm" (¶15).
