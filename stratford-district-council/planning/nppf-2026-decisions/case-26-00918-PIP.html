Case summary

Land off Butts Lane, Tanworth-in-Arden

26/00918/PIP · Stratford-on-Avon District Council · Planning Committee, 9 September 2026 · notice 11 September 2026 · granted

Permission in principle for a minimum of three and a maximum of nine dwellings, in a village washed over by the Green Belt.

Planning record · Committee report · Minutes and Update Report

Cited in: 4. Location shortcuts in the Green Belt; 5. Walking routes and stations

Why this is cited. The mistake: the report applied the S5(1)(j) “well-related to an existing settlement” test, and accepted the unfooted route because “villagers appear to cope”. What should have happened: a washed-over Green Belt village is not a “settlement” and S5 does not apply in the Green Belt (S5(5)), so the scheme should have been judged under GB6/GB7; and limb (iii) should be assessed on the actual quality of the route for all users, which is the test inspectors apply.

Key points

  • S5(1)(j) test applied: "the development would be well-related to an existing settlement and would be of a scale that the local settlement could accommodate" (p.9).
  • The report then assessed the scheme under GB7, including the walking route to the village.
  • Walking route: "the absence of pavements is for very short lengths only and on not especially busy roads and where speeds are low" (p.9). "Villagers appear to cope with this existing situation without any great trouble" (p.9).
